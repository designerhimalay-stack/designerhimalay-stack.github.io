AI agents

AI agents that move the work forward.

An AI agent does more than answer a question. It reads the request, checks your systems, takes the next step, and hands off to a person when judgment is needed.

Claims intake agent

Reads first notice of loss emails, forms, and photos, opens the claim, and routes it to the right adjuster.

Submission triage agent

Pulls broker submissions into your rating workflow, flags missing details, and prepares the file for an underwriter.

Field answers agent

Gives site crews answers from drawings, specifications, and safety documents, without a call to the office.

Maintenance agent

Turns inspection notes and equipment readings into work orders and daily reports your supervisors can approve.

Agentic AI

Agents your team can trust in production.

We build agents with Azure AI Foundry and Microsoft Copilot Studio, inside your Microsoft environment and security controls. Each agent starts as a proof of concept on your own data.

Grounded in your data

Agents answer from your policies, documents, and systems, and show the source for each answer.

A person approves what matters

Any step that commits money, coverage, or a safety decision waits for a person to sign off.

Only the access you grant

Agents work through Microsoft Entra ID permissions, and every action they take is logged.

Tested before and after go-live

We score each agent against real cases before launch, then keep measuring it in production.
